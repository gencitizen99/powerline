Is my property still on the company’s list?

Unofficial community page  •  Lewis map  •  All counties  •  Opposition tracker  •  Developer donations  •  About

On October 13, 2026 Valley Link narrowed the project. Since June they had published 264 “study segments” — a wide net covering every corridor they might possibly use. They have now replaced that with 26 specific route options, and published a shorter list of properties to go with it.

Their property list went from 14,738 parcels down to 9,742. This page lets you check whether a particular property is on the new list. Search by owner name, road or address, or parcel number.

Please read this before you celebrate or panic.
Off the list does not mean safe. It means the company did not include that parcel in this round of outreach. No route has been chosen — the company says it expects to pick a preferred route in early 2027 — and parcels can come back onto the list.
Still on the list does not mean you are losing your land. There are 26 route options and only one will ever be built. Being on the list means your parcel is near at least one option.

What to do next

Whatever your result says, these are open to you right now:

  • Go to the November open house for your county (dates below). This is where the company shows which of the 26 routes run where. Take your parcel number with you.
  • File a comment with the Public Service Commission. The PSC opened Case No. FP Valley Link 26A on September 14, 2026 so people can get on the record before the company files its formal case. There is no deadline posted. File a comment.
  • Check the company’s own map at vltransmission.com/valley-north and turn on the “Parcels” layer. If what you see there disagrees with this page, theirs is the one that counts — and please tell me so I can fix it.

How this was made. “We mapped” is the set of 4,691 West Virginia parcels our earlier analysis found inside the studied right-of-way corridor, using the company’s June 2026 study segments and live parcel data from the WV Property Viewer (mapwv.gov). “Still listed” compares those parcels against the company’s own parcel file published October 13, 2026 (vn-parcels_V4.kmz, 9,742 parcels). The company removed the shared identifier that previously allowed an exact match, so parcels are matched by location — a parcel counts as still listed if the company lists one within about 90 metres of its centre. That method agreed with exact matching about 89% of the time when both were possible in July, so treat an individual answer as a strong indication rather than a certainty, and check the company’s own map before relying on it. This comparison is against the company’s outreach list, not against the new route centrelines themselves — a fresh corridor analysis of the 26 route options has not been done yet. Owner names are from public county assessor records and may be out of date.
